01 / HIMALAYAN CORRIDORS & LOGISTICS

Mountain Gorges, River Spans & High Altitude

Building grid infrastructure across Nepal isn't just theory—it is navigating monsoon washouts along the Trishuli River gorge to transport 63 MVA transformers, assembling 220 kV SF6 GIS bays under mountain barometric pressures at Chilime, and stringing ACSR conductors across steep Himalayan ridges where heavy machinery cannot tread.

02 / URBAN CABLES & SYNCHRONIZATION

Dense Industrial Trenches to 2 AM Grid Charging

From pulling heavy 800 sq.mm XLPE cables through congested Birgunj industrial streets to medium-voltage cabling inside the Nagdhunga Tunnel, true engineering satisfaction arrives in the switchyard control room at 2 AM—watching the synchroscope rotate to 12 o'clock and hearing the 132 kV breaker lock closed as live power surges into the national grid.

03 / BEYOND THE GRID & HOBBIES

Chelsea FC Matchdays & Timeless Classic Rock

Beyond high-voltage switchyard gantries and demanding commissioning schedules, life finds its rhythm through lifelong personal passions. I am an ardent supporter of Chelsea Football Club—watching all of their games, following the club through every competition, and never missing a matchday. When stepping back to recharge, my downtime is soundtracked by timeless classic rock: the progressive atmospheres of Pink Floyd, the guitar mastery of Mark Knopfler and Dire Straits, and the legendary melodies of The Beatles.
