JUN 2023 – PRESENT • SENIOR PROJECT ENGINEER 132 kV GIS / AIS & 800 MM² XLPE UG

Prasauni – Birgunj 132 kV Underground Electricity Transmission Line Project

China Railway No.2 Engineering Group Co., Ltd. (CREGC) • Bara / Parsa Industrial Corridor

01. Project Scope

EPC delivery of a 132/33/11 kV AIS Substation at Prasauni, a 132/66 kV GIS Substation at Birgunj, and a 4 km, 800 sq.mm double-circuit 132 kV XLPE underground transmission line.

02. Engineering Execution

Led engineering design review, GIS hall SF6 bay assembly, urban underground duct-bank civil/electrical coordination, 800 sq.mm XLPE cable pulling, straight-through jointing bays, and cross-bonding links.

03. Field Outcome

MAR 2022 – JUN 2023 • ASSISTANT MANAGER (SUBSTATIONS) 220 / 132 / 33 kV GIS & HYBRID AIS

03. Field Outcome
